    Build report

    What’s in the box and how it closes the water

    What happens when you press Close water

    1. The phone sends the command over home Wi-Fi to the box’s ESP32.
    2. The ESP32 switches relay 1, which takes the 12 V off the valve’s control wire.
    3. The valve motor turns the ball 90° in about 5 seconds, then its own switch cuts the motor.
    4. The valve’s feedback wire tells the ESP32 it is fully shut. Only then does the phone say “Water is off”.
    5. The box’s halo turns red, and every tap device reports zero flow.

    Joining it to the pipe

    • Fit it on the main pipe after the meter or tank outlet, so it controls the whole house.
    • Solvent-weld a ¾" CPVC male threaded adapter (MTA) on each pipe end. Wrap the brass threads with PTFE tape and screw them into the valve.
    • Put a CPVC union on one side, so the valve can come out without cutting the pipe.
    • Keep the actuator on top, and the arrow on the valve body pointing with the flow.
    • Run the valve cable behind the pipe and down to the box, with a drip loop under it.

    Safety and power cuts

    • Only 12 V goes inside the box. The adapter plugs into a normal socket.
    • In a power cut the valve stays where it was. The red knob on top turns it by hand.
    • The box’s round button closes or opens the water with no phone or internet.
    • If Wi-Fi drops, the ESP32 still hears the tap devices over ESP-NOW and can still close the water.
